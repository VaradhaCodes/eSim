  Why it kept freezing: native floating QDockWidget move is genuinely broken on
  your WM. Drag-out works because that's QMainWindow's drag code path; once
  floated, moving relies on Qt's frameless-window move path, which your
  compositor doesn't honor → stuck. Removing my handler didn't help because my

  frameless move.

  move-window primitive — the same thing every frameless app uses, and the only

  reparent fallout, no stuck drag state. Can't break the way the native path
  did.
